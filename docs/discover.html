Two kinds, never mixed. A real-world event is the public record itself changing inside the window (the State listed, sold or redeemed a parcel; the City recorded a vacancy, lien or code case). A first discovery is Property Hunter reading a record for the first time inside the window; the record may be older. Neither says a property is for sale, and neither says the county taxes are paid: those lines are on every card, and they say UNKNOWN when nothing was checked.
