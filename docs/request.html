Records request kit

Why this page exists

The one-year-behind list is not online. It is yours for the asking.

Arkansas property tax has a rhythm. Taxes for a year come due the following October 15. Miss it and the parcel is delinquent at the county: the Collector adds penalty and interest and keeps the list. Stay delinquent long enough and the county certifies the parcel to the State, which then sells it. This site sees the last step every morning (the tax-sale page). The first step, the county's own delinquent list, is a public record that no Garland-area county publishes online, and only the one-year-behind owners are on it: the people a year ahead of the State, and a year ahead of the bulk buyers.

Also worth knowing

Why "not checked yet" shows up on cards

Most Arkansas county Collectors run their online tax search and payment through one State system, CountyPay on Arkansas.gov. It goes down: for maintenance, around the October deadline, and sometimes for a whole day across nearly every county at once. When it is down, this site cannot ask whether this year's bill is paid, so the card says not checked yet instead of guessing. The app keeps trying every twenty minutes and fills the answer in on its own when the system comes back. The State's tax-sale list is a separate system and does not go down with it. Neither system ever shows the one-year-behind list; that is what the request below is for.

Ask for it

The request, ready to send

Under the Arkansas Freedom of Information Act the Collector's delinquent list is a public record. Send this through the county's records portal, or by email or in person at the Collector's office. The office has three business days to respond; there may be a small copy fee. Ask for a spreadsheet, not paper.

Garland County records portal Garland Collector: 501-622-3710

Garland County Collector, 200 Woodbine St, Hot Springs, AR 71901. For other counties, search "county name Collector" for the office; the request wording is the same.

When it arrives

Drop the spreadsheet in and every card updates

  1. Save the file they send (CSV, XLS or XLSX) anywhere on the Mac.
  2. In Terminal: cd ~/Projects/property-hunter && python3 tools/import_delinquent_list.py ~/Downloads/thefile.csv --year 2025
  3. Every property on the list that the hunt already holds gets Taxes: delinquent at the county with the amount; the rest are counted so you know how many owners you are not tracking yet.
  4. The Letters page then puts those owners first: a year behind, not yet at the State, and nobody else is writing to them.
