Property Hunter · Garland County and beyond

Houses people have stopped caring about.

This site reads the public record every morning: the county tax roll, the City of Hot Springs' vacant-house and lien lists, the State's tax-sale inventory, and FEMA. It scores every property, shows you the few worth your time, and hands you one button each.

  1. Today tells you what to do: drive by three, mail ten letters, check your watchlist.
  2. Look opens any property: owner, values, aerials, liens, taxes, and what to confirm before you spend a dollar.
  3. Letter writes the owner, truthfully. You print it. Watch tells you when anything changes.

About taxes, honestly. Two things get checked: whether the State is already selling a parcel for unpaid taxes (checked daily, always right), and whether the county's current bill is paid (read from the Collector's own site). When the Collector's site is down, which happens regularly because most counties share one State system, a card says "not checked yet" rather than guessing, and the app fills it in when the system returns. Values are the county's appraised figures, never sale prices, and nothing here is legal advice.
