# Property Hunter — UX / trust audit, 14 Sep 2026 (before the experience upgrade)

Scope: the eight public pages, the Mac app UI, the data exports they read. Tests: 204 passing before any change.

## Confusing interactions
- Today: the cover, the three steps and the nine cards all compete for "start here"; the hero's three buttons duplicate the card buttons. No "what changed" anywhere on the front door.
- Lookup: one long column with 12+ sections in an order that follows the code, not the buyer's questions (values before "is it inside the Village", taxes after aerials). "Before you buy" is the most important part and is last.
- Tax sale: comps panel and county strip only appear after a county is chosen; "All counties" view has no comps. The Village toggle explains itself only in the header count.
- Watch: a list with facts, no notion of pipeline or state beyond "changed / not changed".
- Letters: filters are a wall of checkboxes; the "why" column is a number (leverage) plus a sentence; the letter preview is only visible in print.
- Scan page: the drawer is good, but the page is a 4 MB single file (embedded rows) and the county select silently loads other counties.
- Radar: three tiers first, product second; the free/paid line is not obvious on the tax-sale page until you hit the gate.
- Mac app: 15 views in a flat nav; the scan view shows stages as a list; no county overview; the morning email is a card under schedule.

## Redundant information / weak hierarchy
- "Taxes" appears in three vocabularies: "Taxes owed", "Taxes:", "State sale". Headers repeat "public records" boilerplate on every page.
- Values row shows appraised/assessed/lien in equal weight; the lien is the one that matters on distress and is visually third.
- Score is a bare number with no breakdown on cards.

## Mobile
- Cards are fine; the lookup's facts grid and the Before-you-buy checklist have small tap targets; the tax-sale rail collapses above the grid and pushes results below the fold; the letter table is 980px wide.
- The cover is a modal on a phone: acceptable, but the "don't show again" control is tiny.

## Dead ends and weak CTAs
- A property with no mailing address has no "Letter" button and no explanation of why.
- Non-Garland counties: "Letter" never appears (no mailing addresses) with no note.
- The scan page drawer has no "watch" button.
- Radar page: the gate copy on the tax-sale page is fine; the Radar page itself never shows an example of the weekly email.

## Terminology
- "Our call" / INVESTIGATE / WATCH / PASS reads like advice. Rename toward research priority.
- "score 100" could be read as certainty or profit.
- "implied" is gone, good; "appraised" still needs the one-line definition where it appears.
- "Deal / Trap" verdict on investigations reads as a recommendation.

## Where inference could read as fact
- Card "why" line mixes facts (City lien $X) with derived signals (building worth less than the dirt) in one sentence.
- Lookup "Parcel class … improved" shows the inferred second letter with a tag, good, but the tag is small.
- Aerial captions say "2023 aerial" but the card never says the building may be gone.
- Tax-sale "delinquent since 2021" comes from the listing page: correct, but nothing says it is the State's figure, not the county's.
- Watch page: "No change since the previous check" could read as "nothing happened" when a source simply did not answer.

## Where a public-record statement could read as a guarantee
- "Owner of record" without the "what this does not establish" note (title, authority to sell).
- "no City lien on this parcel" without "City liens only; mortgages and judgments are at the Circuit Clerk".
- State Lands "for sale" without the litigation-period and title-insurance caveat except deep in the drawer.
- Letter: "the public record shows" is right; add the explicit "does not send mail" reminder near the print button.

## Missing states
- Collector outage is explained on Today only; lookup's tax box says "not checked yet" without the reason.
- fmt$(null) renders "—" with no word; unknown owner renders as empty or "unknown".
- No loading state on the Today cards; empty county on the tax-sale page says "Nothing matches".

## Subscription value
- The paid difference (all counties + CSV + Monday email) is stated but not shown; no sample email on the Radar page; no live "changed this week" example.

## What must not change
- Exclusions (Village, Diamondhead) at ingest and on the pages.
- Evidence provenance and the FACT/OBSERVATION/CALCULATION/ESTIMATE/AI_OPINION/UNKNOWN vocabulary.
- "Not checked" never becomes $0 / none / paid.
- The scanner, adapters, joins, scoring semantics.
