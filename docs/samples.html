How a file is made
1. An address or a county-scoped parcel number is resolved to one verified parcel on the State roll, or the search says honestly why not.
2. Every applicable source is asked through one guarded path: county Collector, State Lands, FEMA, the State road layer, the City of Hot Springs registers, the City's roll copy. A source that does not answer is recorded as unavailable, never as "nothing found".
3. Each question ends FOUND, NOT FOUND or UNKNOWN from recorded evidence only. Unknown stays unknown until a person closes it with a dated, sourced verification.
4. The file lists what a person does next, and the outreach gate says whether a letter could even be prepared. Nothing is ever sent by the system.
