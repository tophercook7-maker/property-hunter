What this is. Every Arkansas foreclosure sale currently posted on an internet foreclosure sale notice service, which Arkansas law requires for a statutory (non-judicial) foreclosure alongside the recorded notice, the mailed notice and four weeks in a newspaper. Each one is joined to the county tax roll by address when the match is exact, so you get the parcel, the owner of record and the county's appraisal next to the sale date.

What it is not. Not a price: no opening bid is published before the sale. Not the lender or the full notice: those are at the county recorder and in the newspaper, and each card links to where to look. Not an outcome: a noticed sale can be postponed, cancelled or cured right up to the hour, and a notice leaving this list says only that. Nobody here is telling you to buy anything.

Sort is by sale date, soonest first. Red dates are within seven days.
