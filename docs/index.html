Property Hunter

Find public-record property signals before they disappear from your radar.

Property Hunter watches Arkansas public records every morning: the county tax roll, the City of Hot Springs' vacant-house, lien and code lists, the State's tax-sale inventory, FEMA and the State's aerials. It connects them per parcel, ranks what deserves a look, and says plainly what is verified, what is inferred, and what nobody has checked.

FindWatches the records continuously.
UnderstandConnects parcel, tax, vacancy, code, value, map and State Lands.
VerifyLabels every fact: verified, derived, estimated, unknown.
WatchKeeps checking for changes.
ActHands you a practical next step.

What it will never do: call an inference a fact, turn "not checked" into "$0", or tell you a purchase is a good idea. Values are the county's appraised figures, not sale prices. Owner of record is not a title opinion. This is research, not legal, tax, appraisal, title or investment advice.
