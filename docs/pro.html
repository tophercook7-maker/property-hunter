Best value per dollar owed, with a building

Appraised value divided by what's owed. The higher, the more house for the debt. Not a guarantee of anything but a place to start.

Where does this come from? The Commissioner of State Lands' public auction feed and monthly county deed reports, the Arkansas GIS Office parcel layer, FEMA, and the State's own aerial imagery. No scraping of anything that asks not to be, no logins.
Is it every delinquent parcel? No. It's every parcel the State is selling: two-plus years behind. A parcel one year behind is still at the county, and only the Collector has that.
Can I buy through this? No. You buy on the State's site with your own account. This tells you what to look at and what it's really worth; the buying, and the 90-day litigation period after, are yours.
Village lots? Hidden by default. Hot Springs Village and Diamondhead lots carry POA dues for life and sell for the debt to bulk buyers. We show them only if you ask.
