When the automated source is unavailable

The legitimate public-record path, in order. Nothing here is sent automatically; a person asks, receives and imports.
  1. Open the county's Collector page on CountyPay (links in the table). If it answers, the poller fills verified bills on its own within 20 minutes.
  2. Open State Lands at cosl.org for anything certified for sale; the site already reads this daily.
  3. Open the county Assessor for deeds and mailing addresses (a browser link on every property file; some assessor sites refuse automation and are never bypassed).
  4. Generate the request kit for the county's one-year delinquent list (Request), send it yourself, and when the spreadsheet arrives import it: python3 tools/import_delinquent_list.py <file> --year 2025. Every parcel on it becomes DELINQUENT — verified, with the list's date, as a MANUAL VERIFICATION on its timeline.
  5. Record a manual result on the property file's "Before you buy" checklist for anything you confirmed by phone or in person.
